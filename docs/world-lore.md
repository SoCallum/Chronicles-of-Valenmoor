# The Sundered Reach of Valenmoor — World Lore

> *"Where the star-spires drift low over emerald boughs, oaths weigh heavier than iron—and the Veil listens to every word."*
> — Archivist Seraphina Dawnquill, Keeper of Oakhaven

## The Realm
**Valenmoor** is an ancient frontier march perched along the western rim of the High Kingdoms. Centuries ago, the First Concord raised floating alabaster **Star-Spires** above the valley to anchor the world's ley-lines and hold back the **Aether Veil**—a shimmering rift of unmaking that devours memory, flame, and stone alike.

In the present age, the Concord is gone. The Star-Spires still drift across the golden clouds, held aloft by failing runes, while the Veil presses closer with every turning moon.

## Oakhaven Bastion
Built across the foaming gorge of the Silverwash River, **Oakhaven Bastion** is the last fortified sanctuary before the wild reaches. Its timber-and-granite towers house the **Wayfarer's Guild**, a fellowship of sworn champions, scholars, rogues, and wardens who chart the fractured realm.

- **The Hearth of Oaths**: A roaring silver-oak hearth where returning wayfarers mend their wounds, cleanse creeping Veil Dread, and record their deeds in the Chronicle Ledger.
- **Master Elric's Curio Forge**: Run by Elric Varn, a sharp-tongued quartermaster with an uncanny talent for acquiring runic blades, warded cloaks, and suspiciously pristine relics *"liberated from abandoned vaults before anyone officially noticed they were abandoned."*

## The Four Reaches (Campaign Chapters)
1. **The Whispering Canopy** (*Chapter I: The Blight of Silverwood*) — Primeval silver-barked woods anchored by the Elder Heart-Tree. Dark thorn-rot creeps from the western hollows, twisting woodland spirits and starving Oakhaven's orchards.
2. **The Sunken Star-Vaults** (*Chapter II: The Drowned Astrolabe*) — Beneath the mirror-still waters of Lake Vesper lies an ancient celestial observatory whose brass rings have begun turning backward, pulling starlight and memory into the deep.
3. **Ashenspire Forge** (*Chapter III: The Ember Covenant*) — A vast obsidian foundry suspended over molten ley-veins where dwarven-elven smiths once forged spire-anchors. Zealots now siphon its heart-flame to break the chains of an elder cinder-drake.
4. **The Crown of Glass** (*Chapter IV: The Shattered Spire*) — The highest floating citadel in Valenmoor, fractured like a prism at the very threshold of the Aether Veil. Whoever reaches its Sovereign Loom decides whether the Veil is sealed, reshaped, or claimed.

## The Four Wayfarer Orders
- **Paladin of the Dawn**: Oathbound shield-bearers who channel solar radiance to guard allies and purge Veil corruption.
- **Arcane Scholar**: Astromancers of the Gilded Archive who decode elder runes, expose illusions, and pierce armor with astral fire.
- **Shadowblade**: Guild scouts and relic-breakers who bend luck, slip past sentries, and strike enemies at the moment they overcommit.
- **Fey Warden**: Keepers of root and mist who walk unharmed through Veil storms and bind beasts with living briars.
